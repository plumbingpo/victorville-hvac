Two situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can save you from a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Los Angeles HVAC System Running Longer

Many mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

Small problems don't stay minor for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a miserable night.
